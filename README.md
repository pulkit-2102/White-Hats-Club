CYBERSECURITY INCIDENT:
One incident I went down a huge rabbit hole on recently was the XZ Utils backdoor. It happened a couple of years ago, but I was just reading a breakdown of it, and it honestly blew my mind.
What’s so crazy to me is that it wasn't a quick hack. It was a massive "long con." The attackers spent years volunteering and writing real, helpful code for a popular open-source Linux tool just to build trust with the creator. Once they finally got admin access, they snuck in a hidden backdoor that would have given them control over millions of servers worldwide.
But the absolute best part is how it got caught. It wasn't stopped by some million-dollar firewall or advanced AI. It was caught by one random software engineer who noticed his computer was lagging by literally half a second when he logged in. He got curious, dug into the code, and unraveled this entire global plot right before it was about to launch.
